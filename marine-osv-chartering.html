SUNDANCE MARINE SERVICES

OSV Chartering

Sundance represents offshore support vessel owners seeking fixtures in West Africa, managing the process from market intelligence through to handover.

How it’s structured

  • Market intelligence on operator and EPC contractor vessel requirements
  • Matching vessel specification and availability against live opportunities
  • Direct engagement with charterers and decision-makers
  • Commercial negotiation and presentation of vessels for nomination
  • Local content and beneficial ownership navigation on the owner’s behalf
  • Handover into technical management support once a fixture is secured

Sundance has been formally authorized, vessel by vessel, as commercial representative for chartering negotiations in West Africa — across AHTS and platform supply vessel classes.
