Ship Management

Sundance provides technical and commercial ship management for vessels operating in West African waters, keeping owners informed and vessels compliant.

How it’s structured

  • Attendance at class surveys, Port State Control inspections and flag State audits
  • Structured reporting — situation reports, operations reports, and incident reporting
  • Crew and technical support coordination
  • Budget oversight and cost control on the owner’s behalf
