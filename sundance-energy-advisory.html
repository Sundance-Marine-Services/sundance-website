SUNDANCE ENERGY ADVISORY

Advisory across the energy value chain

Sundance Energy Advisory is our consultancy business — working with operators, EPC contractors and marine services companies on the technical, commercial and regulatory decisions behind a project.

WHAT WE DO

Advisory shaped around your mandate

Sundance Energy Advisory provides technical, commercial, and regulatory compliance advisory across the energy value chain. Our work is shaped around each client’s specific mandate — a development decision, a bid, an investment case, a compliance obligation — rather than delivered as a fixed service package.

WHO WE WORK WITH

Four categories of stakeholder

Each brings us in at a different point in the value chain.

E&P Operators

Development planning, technical reviews, and business case work.

EPCIC Contractors

Commercial positioning and delivery-risk advisory through the bid and execution cycle.

Investors

Technical and commercial due diligence to test opportunities before committing capital.

Oilfield Tooling & Equipment Vendors

Market positioning and regulatory compliance advisory as they enter or grow within the sector — across tiers, from established multinational suppliers to smaller and emerging local vendors.

METHODOLOGY

The Sundance Change Management Framework

At the centre of this work is the Sundance Change Management Framework (CMF), our flagship methodology for organizations managing structural change. CMF addresses three layers of an organization at once, rather than treating them as separate exercises.

Talent & Technology

Aligning people and systems to new ways of working as the organization changes.

Assets

Matching equipment and physical capacity to the change underway.

Regulations

Building regulatory compliance management into the change itself, rather than addressing it afterward.
