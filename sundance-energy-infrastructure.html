Sundance Energy Infrastructure

Project Advisor for shallow-water
oil-and-gas field development.

We help operators frame development options, engage specialist partners and work through the interfaces between field definition, engineering, fabrication, drilling and marine execution.

A connected development view

Bring the field concept
and delivery plan together.

A development decision depends on more than a single facility or equipment package. Wells, fluids, site conditions, processing, drilling access and the export route must work as one project.

Sundance supports the operator’s decisions through scope definition, concept comparison, partner engagement and interface review.

Advisory scope

From opportunity assessment
to execution interfaces.

Each engagement has an agreed scope, deliverables and decision milestones.

  • 01
    Opportunity & concept assessment
    Review available field information, development options, constraints and data gaps.
  • 02
    Field development & FEED interfaces
    Define the advisory brief and coordinate specialist engineering inputs and scope boundaries.
  • 03
    Fabrication & procurement support
    Review package requirements, vendor capability and delivery assumptions.
  • 04
    Drilling access & rig screening
    Consider site-specific suitability, logistics and mobilisation interfaces.
  • 05
    Marine & installation readiness
    Align transport, load-out, lifting, survey, diving and ROV requirements with specialist providers.
  • 06
    Commissioning & milestone review
    Support handover planning, progress reviews and the next operator decision.

Working with specialists

The right expertise
for the defined scope.

Our partner engagement approach covers field development and engineering design, subsea and topside fabrication, early production facilities, and drilling-rig sourcing.

Partner appointments and equipment availability are established for the specific project. The operator retains investment and statutory decisions; appointed specialists remain responsible for their engineering and execution deliverables.
