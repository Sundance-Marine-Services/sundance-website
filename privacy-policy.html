Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Who We Are

Sundance Technologies West Africa Limited (“Sundance”, “we”, “us”) is the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through this site, we may collect your name, company, email address, phone number, and the details of your enquiry.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this site is used only to respond to your enquiry and to correspond with you about a potential engagement. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data Sharing

We share your personal information only where necessary to respond to your enquiry, and only with our own team members handling that enquiry.

Data Retention

We retain enquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to it and maintain a business record, in line with the NDPA 2023.

Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have rights including access to, correction of, and deletion of your personal data held by us. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your personal data, can be sent to contact@sundance.com.ng.

Project Evaluator and enquiry form

Evaluator inputs are held in browser session memory. The evaluator does not automatically submit or store your assessment on a Sundance server. Save/reopen and report exports use files you explicitly download or open. You decide whether to share them. The enquiry form prepares a draft in your email app; Sundance receives its content when you send the email. The website uses external Google Fonts and is hosted through GitHub Pages; those services receive requests needed to serve their content.
