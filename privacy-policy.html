PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who We Are

Sundance Technologies West Africa Limited (“Sundance”, “we”, “us”) is the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through this site, we may collect your name, company, email address, phone number, and the details of your inquiry.

How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through this site is used only to respond to your inquiry and to correspond with you about a potential engagement. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Data Sharing

Our team uses inquiry information to respond to your request. Zoho Forms processes contact-form submissions, and GitHub Pages serves this website. Those service providers handle information needed to deliver their services. We do not sell or rent personal information.

Data Retention

We retain inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to it and maintain a business record, in line with the NDPA 2023.

Your Rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have rights including access to, correction of, and deletion of your personal data held by us. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests regarding your personal data, can be sent to contact@sundance.com.ng.

Project Evaluator and contact form

The Project Evaluator is free to use without registration, email capture or automatic submission. Its inputs remain in browser session memory. Save and reopen functions and report exports use files you choose to download or open. You decide whether to share those files.

The contact page embeds Zoho Forms. Information you submit there is processed through Zoho Forms so Sundance can respond to your inquiry. Loading the embedded form also sends technical requests to Zoho. You can contact us directly by email or telephone instead.

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Fonts are served with the website files. GitHub Pages receives requests needed to serve the site.

Lawful use and your choices

We use contact information to respond to your request and take steps relating to a potential engagement. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, applicable rights include access, correction, erasure, objection, restriction and data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
