SUNDANCE MARINE SERVICES

Drilling Support

Through our OSV chartering and marine logistics capability, Sundance supports drilling campaigns with the marine services that keep a rig on location and supplied.

Anchor handling and positioning support for mobile offshore drilling units, rig move coordination, and deck cargo and bulk supply logistics to the rig. We work alongside the rig contractor and operator’s logistics team to keep supply runs predictable and deck cargo sequencing safe, and to track vessel availability so a rig move doesn’t stall on marine support. It’s a focused scope — our role is the marine logistics layer underneath a drilling campaign, not the drilling operation itself.

How it’s structured

  • Anchor handling and positioning support for mobile offshore drilling units
  • Rig move coordination
  • Deck cargo and bulk supply logistics to the rig
  • Supply run scheduling and vessel availability tracking
