ABOUT US

A West Africa-focused marine and energy value-chain company

Sundance Technologies West Africa Limited supports marine and energy operators across the region, combining hands-on marine project delivery with technical and commercial advisory.

MISSION

Reliable, safe, innovative service delivery

To provide dependable, safe and innovative marine and energy advisory services that help our clients operate with confidence across West Africa.

VISION

The region’s preferred partner

To be the preferred partner in integrated marine logistics and energy advisory across West Africa, valued for reliability and quality.

CORE VALUES

What guides how we operate

Diligence

Thoroughness and care in how we plan and execute every job — checking the details that get missed when people move too fast.

Integrity

Doing what we say we’ll do, and being straightforward about cost, capability and risk — even when that’s not the easy answer.

Resilience

Staying steady and solutions-focused when conditions on the ground change, which they often do in this region.

Excellence

Holding our own work to a high standard, not just to whatever the client asked for at minimum.
