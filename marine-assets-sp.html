SUNDANCE MARINE SERVICES

Marine Assets S&P

For clients looking to acquire or dispose of offshore support vessels, Sundance provides sale and purchase advisory from initial sourcing through to transaction close.

How it’s structured

  • Asset sourcing against stated vessel preference, age, build year and budget
  • Technical vetting — class history and documentation review, condition survey coordination
  • Commercial and transaction advisory — offer-to-close negotiation, term sheet support
  • Coordination with class societies, flag administrations and surveyors
