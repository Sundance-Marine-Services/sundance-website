FLEET & EQUIPMENT

Fleet and equipment access

Sundance operates an asset-light model by design: rather than carrying the fixed cost of an owned fleet, we give clients access to the right vessel or piece of equipment for the job — through chartering representation and a vetted network of equipment and vessel partners across West Africa.

VESSEL CLASSES

Offshore support vessels, sourced through our network

  • Anchor Handling Tug Supply (AHTS) vessels
  • Platform Supply Vessels (PSV) and Multipurpose Support Vessels (MPSV)
  • Crew and personnel transfer vessels
HEAVY LIFT & DECK EQUIPMENT

Cranes, forklifts and cargo-handling equipment

  • Mobile and crawler cranes, certified and inspected before mobilization
  • Forklifts and yard handling equipment
  • Self-Propelled Modular Transporters (SPMTs) for abnormal loads
  • Rigging gear — slings, shackles, spreader bars, sourced and certified per job
